A LITTLE ABOUT LEAFWISE

More confidence.
More care.

A beginner-friendly plant health assistant that pairs an image classifier with practical, careful next steps.

✳

How the model works

The training script fine-tunes a MobileNetV2 image classifier on labeled PlantVillage-style leaf images. It separates data into training, validation, and held-out test sets and saves measured evaluation results locally.

♡

What a result means

A result is the model's closest supported image category, not a confirmed diagnosis. The confidence score can be misleading on unfamiliar plants, backgrounds, or real-world conditions; lower scores are shown as uncertain.

⌂

Your photo stays local

This starter app processes an uploaded image in the running Flask server and doesn't save the original image. It doesn't send plant photos to an external prediction service.

🌱

A useful next step

Care suggestions are general starting points, not prescriptions. We avoid pesticide dosages; for a worsening plant, confirm the issue with a local agricultural extension service or plant-health professional.
