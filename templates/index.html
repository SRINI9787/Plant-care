YOUR EVERYDAY PLANT CARE

Good care starts with the basics.

Small, steady habits can make a
big difference for your plants.

◉

Water thoughtfully

Check the soil before watering. Water at the base and let excess drain away—most plants dislike soggy roots.

LESS GUESSING, MORE CHECKING
☼

Find their light

Learn your plant's light needs and watch how sunlight moves through your space throughout the day.

LET THE LEAVES GUIDE YOU
✿

Keep roots happy

Use a clean pot with drainage and a growing mix suited to your plant. Healthy roots need air, too.

A LITTLE ROOM TO BREATHE
✳

Feed with care

Fertilizer supports growth; it doesn't cure disease. Follow the product label and your plant's seasonal needs.

FOLLOW THE LABEL
A NOTE ON AI

Helpful hints.
Honest limits.

Once trained, the classifier compares leaf photos with its labeled training categories. Lighting, camera quality, and look-alike symptoms can affect its suggestions. A high confidence score isn't proof of a diagnosis. If your plant is worsening, ask a local gardening or agricultural expert to take a look.
